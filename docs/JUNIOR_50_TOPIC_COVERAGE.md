## Non-negotiable validation requirements

- A topic does not graduate from **blueprint** to **built** until its student input/output loop exists, scientific limits are documented, its task outcomes are tested, and a browser session can complete it.
- A related demonstration must never be labelled full parity with its 3D, molecular, or clinical counterpart.
- Teacher review and actual student usability testing are required before charging for complete-course access.
- Build and deploy tests must pass on Vercel with production environment variables configured.

## Existing curriculum material

The legacy Theory Atlas still contains 68 chapter records, 340 concept cards, and 680 questions. **Only ten legacy cards** had received 200–300 word long-form content before this release. Four new illustrated 200–300 word cards were added for the newly built workbenches. The remaining theory content is still short-form revision content, not complete NCERT textbook coverage.
